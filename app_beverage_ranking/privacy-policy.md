# Beverage Ranking Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 24, 2026

Beverage Ranking (飲評趣, the "App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes, why it is processed, where it is stored, and how you can manage your data.

## 1. Information We Process

The App may process the following information:

- Shops, drinks, branches, ratings, sweetness level, ice level, toppings, prices, notes, tags, rankings, and related records that you create or enter in the App.
- Basic Google account information provided by Google when you sign in, such as account ID, email address, display name, and profile photo.
- Location data provided by your device when you use nearby shop features. The App uses your location to search for nearby beverage-related shops.
- Advertising-related data. The Android free version uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google may process device information, advertising ID, ad interactions, and diagnostic data according to its own policies.
- In-app purchase-related data. The Android remove-ads feature uses Google Play Billing to process purchase, refund, and purchase restoration status.
- App preferences, such as whether onboarding has been completed, theme mode, cloud sync time, and cached remove-ads status.

## 2. How We Use Information

The App uses information to:

- Provide personal drink and shop rating, ranking, search, sharing, export, and restore features.
- Search for nearby shops when you choose to use nearby shop features.
- Sync App backup data to your Google Drive app data folder after you authorize Google Sign-In.
- Display ads, manage ad display behavior, and confirm whether you have purchased remove-ads.
- Maintain App functionality, troubleshoot issues, and improve the user experience.

## 3. Data Storage and Sync

Your rating and list data is primarily stored in a local database on your device.

If you choose to use Google Sign-In and cloud backup, the App stores a backup JSON file in your own Google Drive `appDataFolder`. This folder is provided by Google for the App and does not appear in the normal "My Drive" file list. The App uses this scope only to back up and restore App data. It does not read, browse, or modify other files in your Google Drive.

The App currently does not operate its own backend server to centrally store your rating data.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services. Their data processing is governed by their respective policies:

- Google Sign-In: used for Google account sign-in.
- Google Drive API: used to store backups in your Google Drive app data folder.
- Google AdMob: used to display ads in the Android free version.
- Google Play Billing: used for Android remove-ads in-app purchases and purchase restoration.
- OpenStreetMap Overpass API: used for nearby shop searches. Search requests may include a location-based query.

## 5. Location Data

The App obtains location data only when you use nearby shop features and grant location permission. Location data is used to search for nearby beverage-related shops and calculate distances. The App does not write your location data into cloud backups, but third-party map query services may receive the query location in order to provide search results.

## 6. Advertising and In-App Purchases

The Android free version may display banner or interstitial ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use device information and advertising ID to provide, limit, or measure ads.

If you purchase remove-ads through Google Play, the App queries Google Play Billing for purchase status and caches the remove-ads status locally. Purchase processing, payment, refunds, and billing information are managed by Google Play. The App does not receive full payment information.

## 7. Data Sharing

Except for the third-party services described in this policy, the App does not sell your personal information and does not provide your rating data to a developer-operated backend service.

When you actively use sharing, export, email, or system share features, the content you choose to share will be handled by the app or service you select.

## 8. Data Deletion and Management

You can manage your data in the following ways:

- Clear rating data or remove individual records inside the App.
- Uninstall the App to delete local data from your device.
- Sign out of your Google account inside the App to stop future Google Drive sync.
- Manage the App's authorization and backup data through your Google account or Google Drive-related settings.
- If you purchased remove-ads, restore the purchase through your Google Play account. Refunds and payment disputes are handled according to Google Play policies.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed primarily at children. If you are a minor, please use the App with the consent and supervision of your parent or legal guardian.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy due to changes in features, laws, regulations, or third-party service policies. Updated versions will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
